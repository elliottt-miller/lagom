Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20/02/2026


At Happipola, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or interact with our products and services.


1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Personal Information

Information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name

  • Email address

  • Phone number

  • Shipping and billing address

  • Any information submitted through forms, subscriptions, or contact requests


Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address

  • Browser type and version

  • Device type

  • Pages visited and time spent

  • Referring website or source

This helps us improve performance and user experience.


2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process orders and deliver products

  • Respond to inquiries and customer support requests

  • Send updates, promotions, and marketing communications (only if you opt in)

  • Improve our website, products, and user experience

  • Monitor website performance and security

  • Comply with legal obligations


3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Enhance website functionality

  • Remember your preferences

  • Analyze traffic and usage patterns

  • Improve performance and marketing effectiveness

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.


4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with trusted third parties only when necessary, such as:

  • Payment processors

  • Shipping and delivery providers

  • Website hosting and analytics providers

  • Legal authorities when required by law

All partners are required to keep your information secure.


5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no online platform can guarantee complete security.


6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information

  • Request corrections or updates

  • Request deletion of your data

  • Opt out of marketing communications

You can contact us anytime to exercise these rights.


7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.Please review their policies separately.


8. Children's Privacy

Our products and website are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.


9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
